Dhiraj Kalra

Technical Manager

Video Technical Manager & Media Server Specialist

With 16 years of turning pixels into experiences across live events, immersive shows, corporate productions and anything that demands flawless visuals, Dhiraj specialises in building the technology backbone that makes visual storytelling come alive. He partners with clients from concept through delivery to ensure precision, reliability and impact across LED walls and projection environments.

His expertise spans professional media server platforms including Dataton Watchout 6 & 7, Disguise D3 and Resolume Arena, complemented by switching and processing proficiency with Barco E2, Pixel Hue P20/P80 and Analogway Aquilion. He is specialised with Panasonic and Christie high-end projection systems and has led delivery for high-profile productions including Sharjah Light Festival 2022–2026, MDL Experience KSA, Expo 2020 French Pavilion, Gitex 2021–2025, Anemenia 2026, SoleDXB, Binance Finance Week, and Tanweer Festival. A background as a Senior Motion Graphics Designer and 3D artist strengthens the creative–technical bridge, enabling seamless collaboration with design teams and producers.
