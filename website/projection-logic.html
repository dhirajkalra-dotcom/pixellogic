Projection Toolkit

Plan every surface
with clarity

Projection Logic brings the geometry and on-site tools for professional multi-projector installations into one focused application.

Flat Screen Calculator

Enter screen width, projector throw ratio and projector count. Calculate throw distance, image width per projector, blend overlap zones and total resolution with a live geometry diagram.

  • Throw distance calculation
  • Multi-projector blend zones
  • Lens shift and offset
  • Visual geometry diagram

Curve Screen Projection

Specify curve radius, arc angle, screen height and projector count to calculate coverage and blend overlap across the curved surface.

  • Curve radius and arc angle
  • Per-projector coverage
  • Curved-surface blend overlap
  • Plan-view geometry diagram

Dome and Circular Calculator

Plan dome, cylinder and semi-circular installations. Set diameter, viewing angle and projector count to establish placement, overlap and pixel coverage.

  • 360 degree and semi-circular setups
  • Dome and cylinder calculations
  • Projector placement planning
  • Resolution per segment

Projector Test Pattern

Generate alignment patterns with configurable grids, crosshairs, edge-blending markers and HD, FHD, 4K or custom resolution presets.

  • Multi-projector grid patterns
  • HD, FHD and 4K presets
  • Crosshair and edge-blend markers
  • Full-screen calibration output
